🧰 What This App Does

eve-online-desktop is a small, focused tool. It does not change your game, and it does not touch your settings. It simply keeps a tidy, dated record of the important files and folders that belong to your Eve Online setup.

Every time you run it, the app looks at your Eve Online data folders and makes a copy of your config files and export files. Each copy is given a date stamp, so you can tell exactly which version is which. When a patch arrives and something looks different, you have a clean record to fall back on.

No account logins. No online storage. Everything stays on your own disk, in folders you can open and browse at any time.

📁 How the Dated Backups Work

The idea is easy to follow, even if you have never used a backup tool before. Think of it like making a photocopy of an important document and writing the date on the top. That way, if the original is ever damaged or changed by accident, you still have the older copy showing exactly what it looked like.

eve-online-desktop does the same thing with your data folders. It copies your config and export files, then labels each set with the day it was made. Because the copies are dated, you can quickly scan a folder and see a clear history of your setup over time.

This is especially useful around patch days. Patches can sometimes change how files are read or written, and having an earlier dated copy means you are not starting from scratch if something does not look right afterwards.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Run the backup before you install a patch, not after. The earlier copy is the one that helps most.
  • Keep your dated copies in one predictable place, such as a dedicated folder, so you can find them quickly.
  • If you have a lot of free space, let the dated copies build up over several weeks. Older versions can be surprisingly handy.
  • Make a note in your calendar for patch days, so running the utility becomes a habit rather than an afterthought.
  • Avoid renaming dated folders. The date stamp is what makes the history readable at a glance.
